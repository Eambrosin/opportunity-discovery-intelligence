# Portfolio Demo Walkthrough — IDENTIFY

## Goal

Demonstrate how public market evidence becomes an explainable target-account and contact-validation workflow without turning missing evidence into assumed facts.

## 5-minute walkthrough

### 1. Market setup
Use Medical Aesthetics with the North Italy territory program.

Explain that target profile, commercial territory and vendor/program context are separate configuration layers.

### 2. Discovery quality control
Run Discover & Rank.

Show:
- raw search results
- target-account candidates
- held-back / partner research results
- Qualification Readiness distribution
- territory research coverage

Key point: false positives and research-only results are deliberately held back rather than scored as leads.

### 3. Account Intelligence Workspace
Open a strong practitioner or clinic.

Explain:
- Account Opportunity = attractiveness based on observed fit
- Qualification Readiness = evidence completeness before commercial qualification
- Sales Motion = deterministic next commercial motion
- Buyer Access = observed public access path, not verified purchasing authority

### 4. Enrichment
Run selected-account enrichment.

Show direct website, public address/contact channel, evidence completeness and any technology/treatment signals actually observed.

Key point: “not observed” is not treated as “does not exist.”

### 5. Public Contact & LinkedIn Validation
Run account-specific LinkedIn validation.

Show:
- plausible profile match
- role signal
- location match
- contact relevance/readiness
- held-back homonyms

### 6. ENGAGE handoff
Download ENGAGE Handoff v2.

Explain that the handoff preserves:
- evidence
- gaps
- qualification questions
- validated public contact
- commercial next action

It does not convert uncertainty into buying intent.

## One-sentence portfolio pitch

> I built an evidence-aware Business Development workflow that discovers target accounts, separates fit from research completeness, validates public contacts and hands commercial context downstream without black-box lead scoring.
